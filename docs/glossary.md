# Glossary

- **Allocation rule**: map from a fixed economic history to operational EAD, CCF, LGD, episode, weight, and segment quantities.
- **Economic history**: cash-flow and state history before an operational allocation convention is applied.
- **Joint MoC**: conservative operational parameter choice with a stated simultaneous or risk-functional coverage guarantee.
- **Capital-scale coverage**: coverage of a target risk/capital functional, not necessarily every parameter component.

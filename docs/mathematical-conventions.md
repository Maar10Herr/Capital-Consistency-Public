# Mathematical conventions

- PD, LGD, and CCF are decimal-valued unless a result explicitly enlarges the domain.
- EAD and loss use one consistent currency unit per instance.
- Expected loss and regulatory unexpected-loss capital are distinct functionals.
- Equality means exact equality unless a numerical tolerance is stated.
- Allocation rules operate on an underlying economic history; observational decompositions are not treated as changes to that history.
- The status of every nontrivial claim is stated explicitly.

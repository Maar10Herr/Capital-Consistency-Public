# Regulatory scope

The target is the European Union IRB framework. Binding CRR provisions, regulatory technical standards, EBA guidance, ECB supervisory guidance, consultation material, Q&As, and academic/practitioner claims are distinguished. Basel material is used only where it illuminates or supplies an underlying formula and is not silently substituted for EU law.

## Current legal hinge

Regulation (EU) 2024/1623 (CRR3), principally applicable from 1 January 2025, amended Article 181 so that **for retail exposures** future drawings may be reflected either in conversion factors or LGD estimates. If included in conversion factors, the drawings enter both LGD numerator and denominator; otherwise they enter the LGD numerator only. For non-retail exposures, Article 182(1)(c) requires IRB-CCF to reflect additional drawings up to and after default. In the retail choice, formula-level loss consistency can preserve `EAD × LGD`; the regulation does not state capital invariance.

CRR3 simultaneously introduced/updated PD, LGD, and CCF input floors and requires IRB-CCF to cover drawings up to and after default, use a 12-month fixed horizon, zero-floor negative realised CCF observations for quantification, and apply greater conservatism with stronger expected positive default-frequency/CCF dependence. These nonlinear and statistical requirements are precisely where the simple identity ceases to settle portfolio capital.

## Guidance and transitional uncertainty

- EBA/GL/2017/16 remains the central in-force PD/LGD estimation guidance, including realised LGD/economic-loss construction, multiple-default treatment, and deficiency MoCs.
- EBA/CP/2025/10 is a consultation paper, not final guidance. Its questions explicitly identify allocation, multiple-default, and post-default-drawing issues. It cannot be treated as law.
- The 2024 EBA CRR3 IRB statement discusses implementation prioritisation pending final IRB-CCF guidance; it does not suspend the regulation.
- The May 2026 amended default-definition Guidelines were published but have an application date of 19 October 2026, after this project’s 6 August 2026 access date.
- The ECB Guide release 4.1 of 26 June 2026 states the ECB’s supervisory understanding and expressly does not replace or overrule applicable law. It deleted the prior CCF-estimation section while EBA prepares its guidelines.
